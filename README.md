## Products

### ZuriERP / ZuriPOS
**Run sales, stock and finance from one system built for how African businesses actually trade.**

Many SMEs and distributors still run on spreadsheets, paper receipts and WhatsApp. That means lost stock, unrecorded payments and no real-time view of the business. ZuriERP and ZuriPOS replace that with a single platform covering point of sale, inventory, customers and reporting. Each client gets their own isolated database and infrastructure, so their data stays theirs. Built with Laravel and Vue, and live with businesses in Kenya today.

### Zuri Commerce (field-sales app)
**Gives sales teams in the field the same control as the office.**

Salespeople work away from the shop, where payments, stock and pricing are hardest to track. Zuri Commerce is a Flutter mobile app that records sales and payments as they happen, photographs cheques, tracks live location and enforces price floors on the server, so a rep cannot undersell. Payments are idempotent, so a bad connection never records a sale twice. In active development.

### Zuri Escrow
**Lets two parties who do not know each other transact safely.**

Trust is the biggest barrier to online deals, from general goods to high-value services. Zuri Escrow holds the agreement until each side meets its conditions, releases on milestones, and auto-advances on timeouts so deals never stall. It is built ledger-first on top of a licensed partner custodian, so every movement of value is traceable. Laravel and Postgres. In active development.

### Enara
**A marketplace where reputation is earned and visible.**

Online marketplaces fail when buyers cannot tell good sellers from bad ones. Enara adds a trust layer: verified badges, trust scores and real-time activity, so serious sellers stand out and buyers transact with confidence. Built with Laravel, React and WebSockets. Live at [enara.club](https://enara.club).
